A safe space

The Den is a place to rest, soften and explore at your own pace. Nobody here is judged for who they are, how they sleep, or what they need. You set the pace, you can leave at any moment, and nothing in here is ever asked of you that you have not chosen. Trans, nonbinary and queer listeners are welcome as they are.

Made by one person

Luna Sleep is the work of a single creator, The Primal Luna. Every track is written, voiced, recorded, edited, mixed, produced and released by one person, with no studio or team behind it. That is why it is small, personal and slow to grow, and why the release schedule is steady rather than rushed.

How it sounds

Headphones are required. Most of what follows only works when each ear hears its own channel. On speakers you will miss the effect.
Binaural beatsEach ear hears a slightly different tone, and your brain hears the gap as a slow pulse. Needs stereo headphones.
Solfeggio tonesThe traditional set of meditation frequencies, held underneath the voice as a steady tonal bed.
Orbiting 8D soundAudio that circles you, drifts overhead and moves around your head, so the voice and sounds seem to travel through the room.
Stereo panningThe voice and layers slide from ear to ear, so attention follows the sound instead of staying fixed.
Isochronic pulsesEvenly spaced tones switching on and off, used on some focus and flow tracks. These work without headphones, but are clearer with them.
Hand-built GoldThe Gold albums are composed by hand and do not use a binaural carrier.

The sound design is for atmosphere and relaxation, not medical treatment. Some tracks move sound around you, so listen somewhere you can sit or lie still. Keep the volume low and comfortable.

Consent and what to expect

  • 18+. This app and its audio are for adults. Mature content stays hidden until you choose to show it.
  • You choose what you hear. Every track carries its content level, consent labels and any flags, so you can decide before you press play.
  • Hypnosis works with you. You cannot be made to do anything against your will, and you can stop at any time.
  • Gold is intense. Psychological horror with heightened consent and sharp moments. Best experienced awake, in daylight or early evening.

Safety protocols

  • Headphones, and somewhere safe. Sit or lie down, stationary and private. Never while driving or operating machinery.
  • Check first if needed. If you have a seizure or vestibular history, check with a physician before listening.
  • Tracks end with an exit ramp. Let it finish if you can. Do not stack a second intense track the same night.
  • Emergency Exit is always on screen. The red cross button opens step-by-step help to stop and come back.

About this app

The audio is made by one person: every script, the voice, the recording, the editing, the mixing and the production. None of it uses AI. This app is the only part of Luna Sleep that uses AI.

How much. Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic, wrote the app’s code and layout, and helped word its guides, descriptions and safety text from the creator’s own ideas and materials. The creator decided what the app does, supplied all of the content (the catalog, the schedule, the safety framework and the track information), reviewed it and approved every change. The app does not use AI while you are using it.

Why Anthropic. Out of the AI assistants available, its values were the closest match to the creator’s own at this point in time. The creator also respects the work professional developers do, and plans to hire one for this app when they are able to support a developer the way they deserve.
